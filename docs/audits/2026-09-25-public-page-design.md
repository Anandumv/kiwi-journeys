# Public page design pass — 25 September 2026

The homepage carriage scene remains the lead visual. The other public pages previously reused a dark landscape banner, large white serif title and green gradient. Repeated rounded cards made destinations, FAQ, journal, voucher and contact pages look like generic templates. This pass replaces that shared banner with a light split composition: condensed type beside existing local landscape imagery, a small location caption and generous breathing room. Each page keeps its own subject and copy.

Destinations now use published-tour photographs where available, with open ruled entries and working region links. FAQ, journal, contact, voucher, private-tour and region sections use rules instead of decorative card shells. Tour listings continue to show price, duration and booking links. Tour detail and booking entry headings now share the editorial type scale; transaction controls remain plainly labelled and easy to scan. About and sustainability remain readable long-form pages; legal pages were kept as text documents.

Copy checks found a contradictory 48-hour full-refund claim on tour detail pages. It now follows the existing more-than-72-hours policy. Removed unverified star decoration, fixed 16-person group-size promises that cannot be assured after the vehicle-capacity migration, and removed unsupported cruise-return guarantees. Voucher copy now says delivery follows confirmed payment. Marketing descriptions no longer promise instant confirmation where provider setup is unverified. These edits do not change the underlying cancellation terms, inventory or payment logic.
